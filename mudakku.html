Mudakku Advanced Consultation Master

Source method: Select the Nakshatra occupied by the natal Sun. This module uses the lecturer's Mudakku calculation/mapping and displays the corresponding karmic theme, mirrored present-life theme and specific Mudakku Nakshatra temple. These are traditional source teachings, not scientific or medical conclusions.

Core Mudakku Remedy Protocol

SOURCE — Monthly Lifestyle RemedyEvery month on the Mudakku Nakshatra day
Donate the food associated with the Mudakku Nakshatra to people whose body or mind is restricted/disabled. The lecturer presents this as a continuing monthly practice, not only a one-time remedy.
SOURCE — Transit ActivationBe more careful when a transiting planet crosses the Mudakku Nakshatra.
Continue the monthly donation during such periods. The lecturer suggests using the relevant planet's Hora; for Rahu use Rahu Kalam and for Ketu use Yamagandam. He specifically says not to apply this transit rule to Mandi.
SOURCE — Temple TimingPreferred: Tamil birth month + Mudakku Nakshatra day.
The Tamil birth month is determined by the Sun's Rasi. Take coconut and fruit, perform archanai in your own name and state the Janma Nakshatra.
IMPORTANT RULE 9

Do not bring remedy-visit ritual items home

If the offered coconut is returned, it may be eaten at the temple and any balance distributed there. For this dosha-removal visit, the lecturer says not to bring anything home, including vibhuti/kumkum or remaining ritual items.
IMPORTANT RULE 10

Mudakku Nakshatra remedy ≠ Full Mudakku Dosha remedy

The temples in this module address the Mudakku Nakshatra component. The lecturer explicitly says there is a separate full Mudakku Dosha temple/procedure, involving the broader Bhava/planetary Mudakku configuration. Do not treat one as a replacement for the other.
SOURCE — First PrincipleDo not repeat the karma.
The lecturer treats recognising the karmic behaviour and deliberately avoiding the same behaviour in the present life as the first and most important corrective action.
